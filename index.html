About me

Team work

Team Coaching

I work with your team to reach the goals you've set. No templates — for each team I build a plan around its goals and current situation.

When it helps

  • Conflicts in the team
  • Lack of cohesion or team spirit
  • No shared goals, values or vision
  • Lack of desired results or discipline
  • No strategy — or it isn't working

The result

  • Clear strategy and shared vision
  • Shared values, communication at every level
  • Roles assigned, conflicts resolved
  • Stronger discipline and emotional climate
  • A team that performs better in training and matches
Pricing is always tailored to the team's request.
